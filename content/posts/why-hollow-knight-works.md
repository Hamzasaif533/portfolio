Hollow Knight never tells you where to go, and that's the point. The map you buy is incomplete until you explore, so every corridor feels like a small discovery.

## Three things worth stealing

1. **Withhold the map.** Discovery feels earned when the player draws it themselves.
2. **Use silence.** Long, quiet stretches make the loud moments land.
3. **Reward curiosity.** Almost every dead end hides something.

I'll be thinking about these for my own projects.
